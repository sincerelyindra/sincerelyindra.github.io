Academic projects

Brain-tumor segmentation with LoRA and vision transformers

Developed the 3D Swin-UNETR + LoRA approach for multimodal MRI segmentation; compared volume resizing with centre patches and reported a 0.8572 mean Dice score for the centre-patch experiments.

Robust pricing with minimal demand information

Co-developed an approximate robust-pricing solver and adaptive confidence-interval extension; evaluated 40 store–product pairs over 1,508 rolling windows, with a reported mean revenue ratio of 0.953 versus 0.941 for ERM.

Electric-vehicle routing with vehicle-to-grid services

Co-developed MILP and reinforcement-learning approaches for constrained EV routing with vehicle-to-grid discharge; compared benchmark performance and analysed grid-price sensitivity and Pareto trade-offs.

Strategic bidding in online auctions

Co-developed behavioural feature engineering, choice models, and a DQN auction simulation. Reported 99.5% test accuracy against generated strategy labels and a 65% win rate in the general DQN evaluation.

Path planning for a wheeled robot from aerial images

Integrated U-Net segmentation, modified A* path planning, and PID control in a team project demonstrating aerial-image-based robot navigation in simulation.

Building-material classification from street-view images

Compared VGG16 transfer learning, Canny edge preprocessing, and feature-similarity filtering on 2,516 building images; analysed overfitting, class errors, and Kaggle evaluation results.

Data-driven analysis of Australian suburb profiles

Designed suburb-similarity measures and applied multidimensional scaling and association-rule mining to examine geographic, socioeconomic, healthcare, and community relationships.
