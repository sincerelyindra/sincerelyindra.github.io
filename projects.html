    GPT-2 From Scratch – LLM Systems

    A PyTorch reimplementation of the 124M GPT-2 decoder, with official-checkpoint loading, reported agreement with Hugging Face logits, and autoregressive generation and domain fine-tuning pipelines. The study explains causal attention, tied embeddings, and next-token learning through technical diagrams.
