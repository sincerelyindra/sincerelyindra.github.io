Medical imaging · Parameter-efficient learning

Brain-tumor segmentation with LoRA and vision transformers

Developed the 3D Swin-UNETR + LoRA pipeline for multimodal MRI segmentation and compared whole-volume resizing with centre-patch training.

Reported result: 0.8572 mean Dice in the centre-patch experiments. The study describes the experimental setting and individual contribution.

Robotics · Computer vision

Robot navigation from aerial imagery

Integrated U-Net road segmentation, modified A* path planning, and PID control in a team project for aerial-image-based wheeled-robot navigation.

Scope: A perception-to-control pipeline evaluated in simulation; the study documents its methods and limitations.

Read study

Language modelling · Transformer systems · June 2025

GPT-2 From Scratch – LLM Systems

Reimplemented the 124M GPT-2 decoder in PyTorch, loaded the official checkpoint, and built autoregressive generation and domain fine-tuning pipelines.

Focus: Causal attention, tied embeddings, and reported numerical agreement with Hugging Face logits. Explore the architecture and next-token objective through technical diagrams.

Robust optimization · Decision-making

Robust pricing with minimal demand information

Reproduced a quantile-based pricing framework and extended it with a historical retail backtest and adaptive confidence-interval calibration.

Evaluation: 1,508 rolling windows across 40 store–product pairs; a reported mean revenue ratio of 0.953.
