Data-driven analysis of Australian suburb profiles

Indra Kumar, Ankur Singh, and Santosh Kumar · Team Friday

Project repository ↗

The project tests whether geographically nearby suburbs are necessarily similar. It defines distinct similarity measures for healthcare and accessibility, economic opportunity, and cultural and community characteristics.

Team analysis combining hypothesis-driven similarity measures, visual exploration, and association-rule mining of socioeconomic and demographic features.

Approach

  • Designed three feature-based similarity measures and examined their multidimensional-scaling plots.
  • Compared geographic proximity with socioeconomic and cultural similarity.
  • Mined associations involving occupation, education, employment, family structure, language, and healthcare accessibility.
  • Reported support, confidence, and lift, and visualized relationships as an association network.

Results

The analysis found that geographic closeness alone did not consistently predict profile similarity. The association rules exposed patterns that could inform further investigation of local service needs.

Experimental scope

The discovered relationships are descriptive associations. They do not establish causal effects or validate a policy intervention.
